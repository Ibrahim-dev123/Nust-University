A Societal Mission

NUST was founded to accelerate Pakistan’s scientific and technological advancement, and its purpose has expanded to meet the challenges of a rapidly changing world. From groundbreaking research at the National Science & Technology Park (NSTP) to nurturing ethical leaders across engineering, computing, business, and social sciences, we are dedicated to impact.

Student Life in H-12

A lush 707-acre residential campus at the foothills of the Margalla Hills featuring 16 student hostels, central library, cafes, and 50+ student-led societies.

Arts & Culture

A rich tradition of literary debates, fine arts exhibitions, dramatics, music festivals, and cultural galas celebrating Pakistan’s diverse heritage.

Athletics & Sports Complex

Olympic-standard indoor gymnasium, cricket and football stadiums, tennis and squash courts, swimming pool, and equestrian club fostering sportsmanship.

Admission

Offering extraordinary freedom to explore, to collaborate, and to challenge yourself

Explore the possibilities of a NUST education

Admission to NUST is strictly merit-based through the computer-based NUST Entry Test (NET), SAT, or ACT scores. We welcome talented students from every province of Pakistan and over 35 international countries.

Need-Blind Financial Aid & Scholarships

NUST is committed to ensuring that financial circumstances never stand in the way of a deserving scholar. Over 25% of undergraduate students receive merit scholarships, interest-free loans, or need-based support.
